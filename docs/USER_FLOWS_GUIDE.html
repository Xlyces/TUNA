How to Use the Platform - For Everyone

Welcome! This guide explains how to use the TUNA platform in simple, easy-to-understand language. Whether you're a parent looking for tutors, a tutor offering lessons, or just curious about how the platform works, this guide will help you understand every step.

For Parents: Finding and Booking Tutors

1 Create Your Account

Getting started is easy:

  • Go to the registration page
  • Enter your email address and create a password
  • Select "Parent" as your role
  • Fill out your profile with your name, phone number, and information about your children

2 Search for Tutors

Find the perfect tutor for your child:

  • Use the search page to browse available tutors
  • Filter by:
    • Exam type: DSE or IB (depending on what your child is studying)
    • Subject: Math, English, Physics, or any other subject
    • Price range: Set your budget
    • Rating: See only tutors with high ratings
    • Experience: Filter by minimum hours taught

Each tutor card shows you:

  • Tutor's name and university
  • Their verified credentials (a digital certificate proving their qualifications)
  • Total hours of teaching experience
  • Average rating from other parents
  • Hourly rate
  • Subjects they teach

3 View Tutor Profile

Click on any tutor to see their full profile:

  • Complete profile: Learn about their background and teaching style
  • Credentials: View their DSE or IB exam scores
  • Reputation: See their verified teaching history and ratings
  • Reviews: Read what other parents have said
  • Availability: Check their calendar to see when they're free

4 Book a Lesson

Once you've found the right tutor:

  • Click "Book Lesson" on their profile
  • Choose a time slot that works for you and the tutor
  • Review and confirm the booking details
  • You'll be redirected to a secure payment page

5 Make Payment

Pay securely for your lesson:

  • Pay using FPS (Fast Payment System) or credit card
  • Your payment is held securely until the lesson is completed
  • You'll receive a confirmation email once payment is processed
Payment Protection: Your money is held in a secure escrow account. It's only released to the tutor after both you and the tutor confirm the lesson is complete, or automatically after 72 hours. This protects you if there are any issues.

6 Attend the Lesson

On the day of your lesson:

  • Check your email for the lesson link
  • Click the link to join the video call
  • You can use video, audio, and chat during the lesson

7 Rate Your Tutor

After the lesson is complete:

  • You'll be asked to rate the tutor (1-5 stars)
  • You can leave feedback about the lesson (optional)
  • Your rating helps other parents make informed decisions
  • You'll automatically earn 5 credits for completing a lesson!

8 Use Your Credits

Earned credits? Use them for discounts:

  • View your credit balance in your wallet
  • When booking your next lesson, you can use 50 credits to get HKD 50 off
  • Credits never expire, so save them up!

For Tutors: Getting Started and Teaching

1 Create Your Account

Start your tutoring journey:

  • Go to the registration page
  • Enter your email address and create a password
  • Select "Tutor" as your role
  • Your account will be created

2 Verify Your Credentials

Prove your qualifications to build trust:

  • Go to the verification page
  • Upload a PDF of your DSE or IB certificate
  • Upload a selfie photo for identity verification
  • The system will automatically extract your exam scores from the certificate
  • Submit everything for review
Why verification matters: Verified tutors are more trusted by parents and appear higher in search results. Your credentials are securely stored and cannot be tampered with.

3 Wait for Approval

Our team reviews your application:

  • An admin will review your documents
  • They'll verify your exam scores are correct
  • They'll check that your documents are authentic
  • You'll be notified by email once a decision is made

4 Receive Your Digital Certificate

Once approved, you'll receive:

  • A unique digital certificate (called an SBT) that proves your qualifications
  • This certificate is permanent and cannot be transferred or faked
  • Parents can verify your credentials anytime
  • Your certificate ID will be displayed on your profile
Digital Certificate Benefits: Your qualifications are permanently recorded and verifiable. This builds trust with parents and helps you stand out from unverified tutors.

5 Complete Your Profile

Make your profile attractive to parents:

  • Add the subjects you teach
  • Set your hourly rate
  • Add your availability (when you're free to teach)
  • Write a bio about yourself and your teaching style
  • Upload a professional profile picture

6 Receive Bookings

When parents book lessons with you:

  • You'll receive an email notification
  • View the booking in your dashboard
  • You can accept or decline if needed (most bookings are automatic)

7 Conduct the Lesson

On the day of the lesson:

  • Click the lesson link from your email or dashboard
  • Join the video call with your student
  • Use the chat feature for sharing materials or notes
  • After the lesson, mark it as complete

8 Build Your Reputation

Every completed lesson helps you:

  • Increase your total hours taught (visible to parents)
  • Earn money (80% of the lesson fee goes to you)
  • Receive ratings that improve your average score
  • Build a track record that parents trust
Reputation System: Your teaching history is permanently recorded and transparent. Recent lessons count more toward your reputation, so consistent quality teaching is rewarded.

9 View Your Dashboard

Track your success:

  • See all your upcoming lessons
  • View your earnings
  • Check your reputation statistics
  • See your digital certificate verification link
  • Review your lesson history

For Administrators: Managing the Platform

1 Review Tutor Applications

Help maintain platform quality:

  • Go to the verification queue
  • View pending tutor applications
  • See the exam scores extracted from their certificates
  • Review their uploaded documents

2 Approve or Reject Applications

Make verification decisions:

  • Click "Approve" if documents are authentic and scores are valid
  • Click "Reject" if there are issues
  • Add notes explaining your decision (optional)
  • When approved, the tutor automatically receives their digital certificate
  • The tutor is notified of your decision

3 Monitor Platform Health

Keep an eye on platform metrics:

  • View total revenue (Gross Merchandise Value)
  • See active users (both tutors and parents)
  • Track booking statuses (pending, completed, etc.)
  • Monitor any disputes

4 Manage Disputes

Handle any issues that arise:

  • View dispute details when parents or tutors report problems
  • Review evidence from both parties
  • Make refund decisions if necessary
  • Update dispute status
Note: Payments are automatically released after 72 hours regardless of disputes. This ensures tutors get paid for completed work, but parents can still leave negative ratings if they're unhappy.

5 Export Data

Generate reports when needed:

  • Export tutor certificate data (CSV format)
  • Export transaction logs
  • Export user data (in compliance with privacy regulations)

How the Platform Works

Payment Process

Here's how payments work to protect everyone:

  1. Parent pays for a lesson (e.g., HKD 500)
  2. Payment is processed securely through Stripe
  3. The platform takes 20% as a service fee (HKD 100)
  4. The tutor receives 80% (HKD 400)
  5. Payment is held securely until the lesson is complete
  6. After both parties confirm (or 72 hours pass), payment is released to the tutor
  7. The lesson is permanently recorded

Credit System

Earn and use credits:

  • Earn credits: Get 5 credits automatically when you complete a lesson
  • Use credits: Spend 50 credits to get HKD 50 off your next booking
  • Credits never expire, so save them up for bigger discounts!

Reputation System

How tutor reputation is calculated:

  • Total hours taught: Shows experience
  • Total earnings: Indicates demand
  • Average rating: Shows quality (1-5 stars)
  • Lesson count: Demonstrates consistency
  • Recent lessons weighted higher: Recent performance matters more
  • Fully transparent: All data is publicly verifiable

Digital Certificates (SBTs)

What makes these special:

  • Permanent: Once issued, they can't be deleted or changed
  • Verifiable: Anyone can check if a certificate is real
  • Non-transferable: They belong only to the tutor who earned them
  • Portable: Tutors can share their certificate ID with other platforms
  • Builds trust: Parents know they're seeing real qualifications

Glossary: Understanding the Terms

DSE
Hong Kong Diploma of Secondary Education - the main exam system in Hong Kong
IB
International Baccalaureate - an international exam system
FPS
Fast Payment System - a popular instant payment method in Hong Kong
Escrow
A secure holding account where money is kept until both parties are satisfied
SBT (Soulbound Token)
A digital certificate that proves your qualifications. It's permanent, verifiable, and cannot be transferred or faked
OCR
Optical Character Recognition - technology that reads text from images (used to extract exam scores from certificates)
Credits
Reward points you earn for using the platform. Use them to get discounts on future bookings
Reputation
A score based on your teaching history, ratings, and experience that helps parents find quality tutors
